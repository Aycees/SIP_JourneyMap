Year 1

The Beginning

Adjusting, reconnecting, and learning to serve.

Place

Home / Online Classes

This era was after the pandemic where a whole semester was held online. It was not easy to adjust due to two years without interacting with other people and a transition from High School to College.

Event/Activity

First time watching a school event (U-Fest)

Met my classmates and some of my seniors. It was fun due to how AdDU was prepared to host an event after the pandemic. It was an eye-opening experience where you meet other students and share experiences with them.

Ignatian Value

Being Persons for and with Others

In our NSTP class, we helped a community in Toril. We fundraised money to buy materials for their water pump and to fix their “Purok”. It was a good experience to help other people and see how happy they are with small help from us.

Moment of Becoming

NSTP Class

I realized that many people are in need but they are neglected. Which is why NSTP is a good course for us to be men and women for other people.

Person of Gratitude

HS Friends and College Classmates

My HS friends and I were very close since we had a hard time getting to know other people. My college classmates were important even though we rarely saw each other due to the hybrid setup.

Year 2

Growing Connections

New faces, new responsibilities, deeper participation.

Place

Classroom

This was the time where AdDU announced face-to-face classes. I got to know my classmates, had lunch with them, played activities and sports, and experienced learning with them physically.

Event/Activity

CSSEC

I joined our cluster’s student executive council. I improved my communication and teamwork skills, met my seniors, and became a better person through these experiences.

Ignatian Value

Magis

Becoming a better person and doing more than what is asked.

Moment of Becoming

Spending time with new people

This was the year where we physically met and spent time getting to know each other.

Person of Gratitude

CSSEC and classmates

Without them, my second year experience would be boring and less meaningful.

Year 3

Person of Gratitude

Friends

They motivated me, supported me, and became my comfort and strength.

Final Reflection

The Journey Continues

A short summary of what I learned—and who I became.

Looking back, my AdDU journey taught me that becoming is not only about achievements—it’s about relationships, service, and staying faithful to growth even when things are unfamiliar.

From adjusting in online classes (Year 1), to engaging more fully in campus life (Year 2), to practicing Cura Personalis through discipline and friendships (Year 3), and finally persevering through thesis demands with the help of others (Year 4), I learned to choose Magis: to do more with love, responsibility, and purpose.

What I’m grateful for

Friends, classmates, seniors, and the communities that shaped me and reminded me I don’t grow alone.

What I carry forward

A commitment to be a person for and with others—showing up, helping where I can, and continuing to become.
